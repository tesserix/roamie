Delete your Roamie account and data

You can request deletion here, even if you have already uninstalled Roamie. You do not need to sign in or reinstall the app.

Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email app, write to sales@tesserix.app with the subject Roamie account deletion.

What to include

Contact us from the email address you use with Roamie and ask us to delete your Roamie account and its associated data. If you no longer have access to that email, tell us so we can help verify ownership. Do not send your password, identity documents or complete bank statements.

What happens next

We verify that the request is yours before deleting anything. We then confirm its scope and expected completion date. Account deletion removes your Roamie account profile and associated server-side trip preferences and personal data, including relevant uploaded documents held by our processing services. We coordinate deletion with service providers where necessary.

If you have shared a trip, we explain how deletion affects other participants and remove or anonymize your personal details without deleting another person's account. Deleting Roamie does not delete your Google or Apple account, or automatically close accounts you use with other Tesserix products.

Records and copies that may remain

Limited security, fraud-prevention, audit or legally required records may need to be retained. Backup copies expire through the backup retention process. We explain any applicable retention exception and period in our response. Files you exported or sent to another person are outside our control.

Your device also stores wallet records and trip memories. After your request is complete, clear Roamie's app data or uninstall the app on each device to remove local copies. Signing out alone does not delete your data.

Delete selected data instead

You can use the same email address to request deletion of particular server-side documents or trip data while keeping your account. Describe the item without attaching more sensitive information.
