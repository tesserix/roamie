Your privacy in Roamie

Roamie is a travel companion from Tesserix Pty Ltd. This policy explains what happens to your information when you translate a conversation, plan a trip or use another Roamie feature. Last updated: 21 October 2026.

Delete your Roamie account and data · Contact us

Information you choose to use

  • Your account: your verified email address, display name and account identifier are used to sign you in and keep your data separate from other accounts.
  • Translation: text you enter, voice recordings you start and images you select are sent to our online services when you request translation. Recent conversation turns may be included to understand a reply. Conversation turns remain available in the current app session. Roamie also keeps the text and translation of each conversation turn, typed translation and sign line, with an account ownership record, to answer repeated phrases faster and improve translation while allowing your contributions to be found for deletion. Text that looks like an email address or a long number is not kept.
  • Trips and nearby places: destinations, dates, budgets, interests and optional food or accessibility preferences help us plan and personalize your trip. Your location is sent when you use nearby features. You can decline location access and enter information manually where the feature supports it.
  • Receipts and statements: files you choose are uploaded for text recognition and extraction. They may contain financial or personal information. Review the extracted amounts before saving them, and avoid uploading information you do not need Roamie to process. Roamie does not connect to your bank or move money.
  • Shared group expenses: when you create or join a group, participant names, optional email and WhatsApp contacts, expenses, split amounts and recorded repayments are stored on our servers and visible to group members. Only add another person’s contact details with their permission. An explicit email invitation lets the verified owner of that email join the group; saving a contact alone does not grant access. Home-currency conversions use reference exchange rates, not a bank payment service.
  • Expense reminders: an authorized group member can review and request an email reminder for a balance with shares unpaid for at least 30 days. We use Resend to process the recipient email address and reminder content. A reminder can also be opened in WhatsApp for the user to review and send; WhatsApp then handles the message under its own terms. Contacts are not used for advertising. Provider acceptance is not a guarantee that an email was delivered.
  • Memories: selected photos stay on your device for albums and PDF exports. Creating a video uploads the selected photos and any chosen music temporarily for rendering. Temporary rendering files are removed after the job finishes.
  • Operation and support: we record technical events, account and request identifiers, model usage and audit events to operate the service, understand costs and investigate problems. We receive any details you send to support.

Online processing and service providers

Connected features use Tesserix services hosted on Google Cloud, our identity service, AI models on Google Cloud, TypeSafe JEV for quality review, and document-recognition services for uploaded files. Travel features use providers for places, maps, weather, travel information and currency rates. These providers receive the information needed for the feature you request, such as a location, destination, text or selected file. Before your first AI request, Roamie asks permission to share the needed content with these AI services. You can decline or withdraw future AI sharing in Profile & settings → AI data sharing. Withdrawal does not erase previously sent information; use account deletion for that. AI output can be wrong; confirm important travel, safety and financial information independently.

We use this information to provide the features you request, maintain your account, keep the service secure and respond to support requests. Roamie has no advertising, does not use advertising identifiers and does not sell your personal data. Sharing a trip or exported file is your choice; recipients can keep their own copies.

Storage and retention

Roamie stores wallet records and trip memories on your device. Account records, connected trip preferences, shared trip information and audit records also exist on our servers. Uploaded documents may remain in the document-processing service according to its retention settings. Signing out or deleting a local trip does not by itself erase server records. Kept translations have an account ownership record for deletion. Removing your account removes your contributions; a phrase independently contributed by other travellers may remain for those travellers. Avoid sharing personal details that are not needed for translation.

We keep account and feature data while it is needed to provide the service, then handle deletion through the process below. Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations, and backup copies may remain until their normal expiry. We will explain any applicable exception and retention period when handling your request. Our main hosting region is India; service providers may process information in other countries.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline optional camera, microphone, photo, location and notification permissions in your device settings. You can review selected files and generated results before saving or sharing them. To initiate deletion of your Roamie account and associated personal data inside the app, open Profile & settings → Delete my account → Request account deletion. Our team at sales@tesserix.app completes requests and emails confirmation within 30 days, explaining any required retention exception. To access or correct your account information, or request deletion without the app, email sales@tesserix.app. The account-deletion page explains what to send and what will be removed. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a request.

Age and security

This release is intended for people aged 18 and over. Connected requests use HTTPS. Access to customer data is restricted to the systems and people who need it to operate the service. No system can guarantee absolute security.
